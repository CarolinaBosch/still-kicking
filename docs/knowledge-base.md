# Still Kicking: what this call is

Still Kicking is a short daily check-in call for older adults who live at home. A friendly voice nurse asks the same few questions every day: how you feel, any pain, eating and drinking, swelling, mood, and your medicines. It takes about two minutes.

## Who sees your answers
- Your nurse sees every check-in on the nurse dashboard.
- Your caregiver (the family member or friend you chose) gets a short plain-language summary after each call.
- Nobody else. Your answers are not sold or shared with advertisers.

## It is not an emergency service
Still Kicking does not call 911 for you. If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, a bad fall, signs of a stroke, fainting, or bleeding that won't stop, hang up and call 911 right away. If you mention one of these during a call, we alert your nurse and caregiver at once, but you still need to call 911 yourself.

## What happens with your answers
After each call the system looks over your last two weeks of check-ins to spot slow changes, like swelling that keeps coming back or missed doses of a medicine. Anything worth a look goes to your nurse, who decides whether to call you, your caregiver or your doctor. The call itself never gives medical advice or changes your medicines.

## When calls happen
- Once a day, at the time you and your nurse chose. The nurse tells you the next call time at the end of each call.
- If you miss a call, we try again later. After three missed calls in a row, your nurse and caregiver are told so someone can check on you.
- To change your call time, tell the nurse during the call, or ask your caregiver or your nurse to change it. The voice nurse passes the request on; it cannot change the schedule itself.

## Medicine questions
The check-in nurse cannot tell you whether to take, skip or change a medicine. She passes your question to your real nurse, who will follow up.

## Privacy basics
- Calls are used only for your care.
- You can stop the daily calls at any time by telling your nurse or caregiver.
- The voice nurse will not share your health details with anyone else who answers the phone.
