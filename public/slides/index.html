Walt, 83, at home

Heart failure, strong opinions, zero interest in moving. Small changes at home can turn serious fast.

💌

Maria, his worried wife

Asks "did you take your pills?" four times a day. Gets "yes, dear" back. Wants to know what actually changed.

🏁

The finish line

After every call, the nurse and caregiver know what changed in plain words, and get alerted right away if it's serious.

All patients in the demo are synthetic.

Under the hood (it's mostly Python in a box)

🎙️ ElevenLabs: the charm

  • Voice agent in Carolina's cloned voice, Claude as its brain
  • Real tool calls: emergency alert, check-in record
  • Knowledge base, Spanish switching, tuned turn-taking

📦 Daytona: the brains in a box

  • Patient records live in a Daytona records sandbox
  • A fresh sandbox runs our Python engine on every call, about 1 s
  • 36 engine tests run inside a sandbox from the dashboard

Someone talks → tool → Daytona → said out loud

Cheeky, not reckless

🙅

Never diagnoses

It's a nurse check-in, not Dr. Google. Medicine questions go to the real nurse.

📞

911 comes first

It never says help is coming. It tells them to call 911 and gets the humans.

🧸

No real grandmas were harmed

Synthetic patients only. A prototype, not a medical device. Real use needs clinical validation and compliant vendors.
